Important Safety Notice

DE NOW is intended for commercial service problems such as vehicle breakdowns, electrical problems, plumbing problems, locksmith services and other urgent assistance.

If someone is in immediate danger or needs police, ambulance or fire assistance, contact the appropriate official emergency service instead of relying on DE NOW.
